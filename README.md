# 💫 About Me

I'm a **Senior Frontend Engineer** with 15+ years of experience building modern, scalable, and high-performance web applications.

I specialize in frontend development using **React.js, Next.js, TypeScript, Remix, Angular, and JavaScript**, with a strong focus on frontend architecture, reusable component systems, performance optimization, and maintainable application development.

Throughout my career, I've worked on a range of products and digital experiences, from AI-powered content platforms to large-scale business applications. My experience includes server-side rendering (SSR), static site generation (SSG), search engine optimization (SEO), responsive interfaces, and building reusable design systems.

I've contributed to projects and digital platforms for organizations including **STC, Invest Saudi, Derayah, and Riyadh Airports**, alongside product development experience at companies in Dubai and Riyadh.

I enjoy solving complex frontend challenges, improving application performance, establishing scalable development practices, and turning product requirements into reliable user experiences.

**Areas of focus:**
- Frontend architecture and scalable application development
- React.js, Next.js, Remix, Angular, and TypeScript
- Server-side rendering (SSR) and static site generation (SSG)
- Performance optimization and SEO
- Reusable components and design systems
- Responsive web development and user experience
- AI-powered product interfaces
